Telecaller — Job Summary

As a Telecaller at Gurukul Classes you will be the first point of contact for prospective students & parents. You’ll handle inbound/outbound calls, answer queries, schedule campus visits, and convert leads into admissions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Make outbound calls to prospective students & parents.
  • Handle inbound admission inquiries via phone & WhatsApp.
  • Explain course details, fees, batch timings, and hostel information.
  • Maintain leads, follow-ups and update CRM/Excel daily.
  • Coordinate with counselors and admission team for demos & visits.

Requirements

  • 12th pass (Graduation preferred).
  • Excellent communication in Marathi & Hindi; basic English.
  • Good phone manners, persuasive & polite.
  • Basic computer skills (Excel, WhatsApp).
  • Experience in education sales is a plus.

Salary: Fixed + performance incentives. Training provided.
